No-Show & Readmission Risk: From Analysis to a Business Case

Two public healthcare datasets, analyzed for cost impact, then translated into a one-pager and user stories a clinic ops team would actually act on.

RoleBA / Data Analyst
DatasetsKaggle No-Show · UCI Diabetes Readmit
MethodEDA · KPI Design · User Stories
Records~110K + ~100K

The business problem

Two related but distinct revenue leaks: patients who don't show up for scheduled outpatient appointments (wasted slot capacity), and patients readmitted within 30 days of a hospital stay (cost and, under CMS penalty programs, a direct reimbursement hit). Both are usually treated as "just build a churn model" problems — the BA work is turning that model into KPIs and workflows an ops team will actually track.

Datasets

Dataset 1 — No-shows~110,500 outpatient appointments (Kaggle, Brazil public health system). Fields: scheduling lag, SMS reminder flag, age, neighborhood, chronic conditions (hypertension, diabetes, alcoholism), scholarship status. Baseline no-show rate in the raw data: ~20%.
Dataset 2 — Readmissions~100,000 hospitalizations across 130 US hospitals, 1999–2008 (UCI/Kaggle Diabetes Readmission set). 55 features spanning diagnoses, medications, length of stay, and prior visit history; target is readmission within <30 days, >30 days, or none.

BA artifacts produced

One-pager business caseEvery no-show is a wasted appointment slot with real opportunity cost; every avoidable 30-day readmission risks a CMS reimbursement penalty on top of the direct cost of care. Neither is currently flagged before it happens — both are identified only after the fact, in monthly reporting.
Key EDA findingsNo-show risk rises sharply as the gap between scheduling date and appointment date grows, and drops noticeably when an SMS reminder was sent — both are levers ops can actually pull, unlike demographic fields.
User stories (TO-BE dashboard)"As a clinic scheduler, I want appointments flagged as high no-show-risk at booking time, so I can offer a reminder call instead of just an SMS." / "As a discharge planner, I want a readmission-risk score visible before discharge, so I can route high-risk patients to a follow-up call within 48 hours."
KPI definitionsNo-show rate by scheduling-lag bucket; reminder-to-attendance lift; 30-day readmission rate by discharge diagnosis category; readmission-risk-flagged patients who received a follow-up contact within 48 hours (process adherence, not just outcome).
